Pokémon Battle - three looks to pick from

Will: pictures only. Classic turn-based battle, no maths in it. Same scene in every picture: his Gold-card Charizard against a Blastoise, his move list, his team of six along the bottom. The sprites are the real animated ones, streamed live from PokéAPI exactly as Sudoku does; nothing is saved into the repo.

How it works. Only Pokémon he caught in Pokémon Sudoku can fight. Card grade is power: Normal, Shiny, Full Art, Gold. Four moves each, real types, water beats fire. First to knock out all six wins. No timers, no maths.
What I need: a letter (A, B or C). Part 2 shows the team-pick screen and the leader ladder, in look A only.
