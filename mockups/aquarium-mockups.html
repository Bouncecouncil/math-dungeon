Part 1 — the park, phone held sideways

Each picture below is one screen of the game, full size, exactly as it would appear on a phone turned sideways. All three contain the same things so you are comparing paint, not content: a tank with fish in it and room for more, an empty plot with a price on it, the ticket desk with people lined up, the upgrade podium, a locked door to the next area, the kid walking, and a yellow arrow trail on the ground. Notice the park runs off all four edges — the camera follows the kid and there is always more park past the screen. No black space, no grid.

A

SUNNY REEF

Bright midday park. Warm tan sand, turquoise water, tropical planting, saturated colour and soft shadows. This is the closest match to the game Lennon already plays.

B

CHUNKY CARTOON

Thick black outlines, flat bold colour, fat rounded shapes, high contrast. Reads like a modern console kids game. Loudest and clearest of the three on a small phone screen.

812 × 375 px — shown actual size

SOFT STORYBOOK

Painterly pastel gradients, gentle edges, no hard lines. Picture-book warmth and charm, lower contrast. Calmest of the three and the most grown-up looking.
